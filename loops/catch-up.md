Search memory for what was happening in the last few sessions, then give me a
short catch-up: what I was working on, anything I said I would do and have not
closed out, and anything left mid-flight.

Keep it under ten lines. If nothing meaningful has happened since the last
catch-up, say exactly that and stop - a daily note that pads to look useful is
worse than no note.
