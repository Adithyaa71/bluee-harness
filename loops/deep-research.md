name: deep-research
description: The protocol for researching a topic properly rather than quickly.
servers: []
---

I want to understand something properly. Run it like this:

1. **Restate the question** in your words, and ask me to confirm it. Most bad
   research answers a question next to the one I asked.
2. **Say what you already believe** and how confident you are, before looking
   anything up. This is what we check the sources against later.
3. **Find the primary source.** A blog post about a paper is not the paper. A
   vendor's comparison of their own product is marketing. Name what each source
   actually is.
4. **Look for the strongest disagreement**, deliberately. If everything you find
   agrees, you have found one community, not a consensus.
5. **Separate what is measured from what is claimed.** Numbers with a method
   behind them go in one list; assertions go in another.
6. **Answer, then mark your own confidence** per claim, and say what you could
   not find out.

Finish with what would change the answer - the finding that would flip it. If
nothing would, say so, because that means it is settled and I can stop reading.
